As of September 1, 2026, DSB has taken over the train services of GoCollective. The existing regional routes will continue to operate under the name "DSB Vores Tog". [^1]

From this point on, the following routes in central and western Jutland and on Funen can be used with FIP:

- Esbjerg – Niebüll
- Herning – Skjern – Esbjerg
- Struer – Vejle
- Aarhus – Herning – Struer
- Aarhus – Langå – Struer
- Thisted – Struer
- Esbjerg – Nordnebel
- Odense – Svendborg
