date: 2026-10-04
title: "DB Regio services in the Neckar Valley now available with FIP"
description: DB Regio Stuttgart has been fully integrated into DB Regio Baden-Württemberg since 1 August 2026, meaning that trains in the Neckar Valley can now be used with FIP.
---

On 1 August 2026, the Neckar Valley network, formerly operated by DB Regio Stuttgart GmbH, was fully integrated into DB Regio Baden-Württemberg and can therefore be used with FIP. [^1]

The following services can now be used with FIP:

- **RE 6**: Tübingen - Stuttgart
- **RE 10a**: Heilbronn - Mosbach-Neckarelz - Heidelberg - Mannheim
- **RE 10b**: Heilbronn - Sinsheim (Elsenz) - Heidelberg - Mannheim
- **MEX 12**: Tübingen - Stuttgart - Heilbronn
- **MEX/RB 17a**: Stuttgart - Mühlacker - Pforzheim - Karlsruhe/Bad Wildbad
- **MEX/RB 18**: Tübingen - Stuttgart - Heilbronn - Osterburken
- **RB 71**: Mühlacker – Bretten – Bruchsal

More information about the Neckar Valley network is available on the [DB Regio website](https://regional.bahn.de/regionen/baden-wuerttemberg/ueb/partner/netz-neckartal).

[^1]: [DB Regio Stuttgart GmbH](https://regional.bahn.de/regionen/baden-wuerttemberg/ueb/partner/db-regio-stuttgart)
