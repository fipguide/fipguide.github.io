## FIP Information

In North Macedonia, rail transport is operated by the state railway company Železnici na Republika Severna Makedonija (ŽRSM).

## Interesting

North Macedonia has a 925-kilometre standard-gauge rail network, which can be used at a maximum speed of 100 km/h. Only the Tabanovci – Gevgelija line has an automatic train protection system. In the past, there were also narrow-gauge railways, but none are in use anymore. There is currently no international passenger rail traffic, although there are new construction and expansion projects. Rail transport has a relatively low priority in the country. [^1]

There is no regular timetable, but only individual trains operating at longer intervals. Many connections start or end in Skopje. The most important route from a transport perspective is the north-south line through Skopje, although it does not offer particularly notable tourist features. Rail transport as a whole has a low priority in the country — the bus system dominates local public transport.

### Serbia

Currently, there are no passenger trains between North Macedonia and Serbia.

### Greece

There is no scheduled passenger rail service between North Macedonia and Greece. The historic connection between Thessaloniki and Gevgelija/Skopje is currently not in operation. Border crossings therefore cannot be crossed by train; alternative options include long-distance buses via the Balkan routes.
