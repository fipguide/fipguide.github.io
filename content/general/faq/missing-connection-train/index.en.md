---
title: What happens if I miss my connecting train?
weight: 17
---

It can happen that you miss your connecting train.

If you have a flexible ticket and the next train does not require a reservation or additional fee, you can simply take the next train, provided it is also covered by FIP. Check the corresponding [operator page](/operator) for this. On some operator pages we have also provided further information about missed connections and the respective regulations.

If the connecting ticket is bound to a specific train and you do not have a through ticket, there are two options for continuing your journey:

- HOTNAT ([More information](#hotnat))
- AJC ([More information](#ajc))

HOTNAT and AJC are regulations for continuing your journey with a train and do not provide for reimbursement of hotel or taxi costs.

In addition to the options mentioned above, you also have [statutory passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/rail/index_en.htm), provided it is a joint transport agreement (e.g., a through FIP reduced ticket or a transfer within the scope of a FIP Coupon).

Under passenger rights, the validity period of each field and FIP Coupon can also be extended by one day if the original journey can no longer be completed within its original period of validity due to a missed connection or train cancellation. To do this, station staff at the station where the connection was missed must make a corresponding note on the FIP Coupon.
