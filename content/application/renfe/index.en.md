title: Renfe
---

Beneficiaries (1): Spouses/registered partners and children. Beneficiaries (2): Spouses/registered partners/widows or widowers and children or orphans.

Children or orphans eligible for FIP are:

- Ages 4 to 24: They must live in the household of the railway employee and must not have paid employment.
- From age 25: Children or orphans with a disability may also remain eligible.
