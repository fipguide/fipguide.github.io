## FIP Coupon

{{< fip-validity-table type="fip-coupon" >}}

Active employees receive coupons beyond FIP at the TCDD (Turkey), SNCFT (Tunisia), ONCFM (Morocco), CFS (Syria), ER (Egypt), SNCFA (Algeria) and HSH (Albania). Relatives of active employees only receive coupons for the ER (Egypt).

## FIP Reduced Ticket

{{< fip-validity-table type="fip-reduced-ticket" >}}

Active employees and their relatives receive 50% beyond FIP at the TCDD (TTurkey), SNCFT (Tunisia), ONCFM (Morocco), CFS (Syria), ER (Egypt), SNCFA (Algeria) and HSH (Albania). Relatives of active employees also receive a 50% discount.
