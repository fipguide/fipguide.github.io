The Željeznice Republike Srpske (ŽRS) is the railway operator of the Republika Srpska Federation in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Alongside it, the country also has [Željeznice Federacije Bosne i Hercegovine (ŽFBH)](/operator/zfbh) of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. The ŽRS currently operates only a minimal regional train service on its 418 km rail network.

## Summary

- FIP 50 / FIP 75 Tickets and FIP Coupons are accepted.
- The sale of FIP discounted tickets takes place only on-site.

## Validity of FIP Tickets

{{< fip-validity type="fip-coupon" status="valid" >}}
{{< fip-validity type="fip-reduced-ticket" status="valid" subtitle="FIP 50 / FIP 75" >}}

FIP Coupons and FIP 50 / FIP 75 Tickets are valid on ŽRS services. For cross-border journeys (currently not offered), either a through FIP 50 / FIP 75 Ticket or FIP Coupons from both countries would be required. For journeys across the entity border (also currently not available), either a through FIP 50 / FIP 75 Ticket or an FIP Coupon of ŽRS in combination with an FIP 50 / FIP 75 Ticket from the entity border would be required.

We do not know whether FIP tickets can be purchased on the train. Since many stations do not have a ticket office, however, it is reasonable to assume that at least regular tickets are sold on the train.

## Discounts

Children up to and including 3 years travel free of charge if they do not require an extra seat. Children under 12 years receive a 50% discount on the regular fare regardless of FIP.[^1] An overview of all discounts outside FIP is available on the [ŽRS Website](https://www.zrs-rs.com/putnicki-saobracaj/opsti-uslovi).

{{% highlight tip %}}
For travellers crossing several Balkan countries, the _Balkan Flexipass_ may be worthwhile. It allows unlimited travel on the rail networks of:

- Bosnia and Herzegovina (ŽFBH, ŽRS),
- Bulgaria (BDŽ),
- North Macedonia (MŽ-Transport),
- Greece (HT & Attica),
- Montenegro (ŽPCG),
- Romania (CFR Calatori & Regiotrans),
- Serbia (SV),
- Turkey (TCDD).

[Further information on the ŽPCG website](https://zpcg.me/en/medjunarodni-prevoz/povlastice)
{{% /highlight %}}

## Special Tariff Conditions

### Rail Replacement Services

FIP discounts apply on rail replacement buses if they replace a train on which FIP would have been valid.

## Recommendations

{{% highlight tip %}}
The ŽRS operates only very few trains within the Republika Srpska entity. Banja Luka, the capital of the Federation, is connected both to the border with Croatia and to the entity border. Cross-border and inter-entity trains are currently not offered, and the timetable consists of one or two trains per day on each route. The rolling stock is also somewhat old.
{{% /highlight %}}
