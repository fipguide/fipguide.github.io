Ticket offices of ŽRSM can be found at the following stations:

- Skopje
- Volkovo
- Gjorche Petrov
- Kumanovo
- Veles
- Negotino Vardar
- Gevgelija
- Bitola
- Prilep
- Shtip
- Tetovo
- Gostivar
- Gradsko
- Kichevo
- Tabanovci
  {.o-section--columns-3}

International tickets are issued at international ticket offices.

If you do not have a ticket, the fee for access to the waiting room or platform is 26 MKD.[^1]

{{% booking-section "fip_50" %}}

## FIP 50 Tickets

At the ŽRSM ticket office, FIP 50 tickets can be purchased for journeys on trains operated by ŽRSM.

The ŽRSM website publishes a [price overview for standard fares](https://mzt.mk/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/%D0%A6%D0%95%D0%9D%D0%90-%D0%9D%D0%90-%D0%92%D0%9E%D0%97%D0%95%D0%9D-%D0%91%D0%98%D0%9D%D0%95%D0%A2.pdf). The relevant route can be identified using the table to determine the fare (in denars). We assume that the price for FIP-reduced tickets is 50% of the standard fares.

## Reservations

Seat reservations can be made at the ŽRSM ticket office for a fee of 27 MKD per seat.[^1]

The reserved seat must be occupied no later than 15 minutes after the departure of the train from the station at which the reservation was made. If the seat is not claimed within this time, it is considered available to other passengers.[^2]
