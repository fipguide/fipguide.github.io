Ticket offices of the ŽFBH are only found at the most important stations. A list of the ticket offices is available at the bottom of the [ŽFBH website](https://www.zfbh.ba/en/kontakt/).

{{< highlight inofficial >}}
At some ticket offices, tickets may only be paid for in cash.
{{< /highlight >}}

{{% booking-section "fip_50" %}}

## FIP 50 Tickets

At the ŽFBH ticket office, FIP 50 tickets can be purchased for journeys on trains operated by ŽFBH within Bosnia and Herzegovina and for journeys to Croatian Ploče.

{{< highlight inofficial >}}
FIP is not always known on-site and some tickets are sometimes issued with other discounts. [^1]
{{< /highlight >}}
