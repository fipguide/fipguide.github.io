Ticket offices of the ŽRS are only found at the most important stations.

{{% booking-section "fip_50" %}}

## FIP 50 Tickets

At the ŽRS ticket office, FIP 50 tickets can be purchased for journeys on trains operated by the ŽRS within Bosnia and Herzegovina.

{{< highlight inofficial >}}
It is reasonable to assume that the ticket offices selling Interrail tickets also offer FIP tickets. These offices are listed on the [ŽRS website](https://www.zrs-rs.com/putnicki-saobracaj/specijalne-ponude).
{{< /highlight >}}
