As an employee in Germany, you can use this tool to calculate the taxable value of your travel benefits.

Currently, the tax calculator is only available to employees in Germany. Different tax regulations may apply in other countries. If you have information about this, we would appreciate a [message](/contact) from you.

{{% highlight important %}}
We accept no liability or guarantee for the accuracy of the calculated data. The results are for orientation only, are non-binding, may contain errors, and do not constitute tax advice.
{{% /highlight %}}

## Instructions

{{% highlight tip %}}
Before using the tax calculator, familiarize yourself with the general principles of FIP taxation. You can find these on the [FIP Application > Taxation](/general/fip-validity/#taxation) page.
{{% /highlight %}}

1. Select the number of persons for whom you want to calculate international coupons.
2. Select the coupons and the number of fields you need (the same class and quantity are applied to each person, where possible).
3. Add national discounts and any additional taxable non-cash benefits.

The total taxable amount is then calculated.

In [Planning & Optimization](#planning-optimization), you can reduce your tax impact by distributing amounts across multiple months.
